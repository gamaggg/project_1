# Technique library M1–M20, transitions and choosing

Part of the by-motion skill. Read together with its SKILL.md.

## Technique library

Compiled from an analysis of ~100 motion-design references. A technique is a structure of movement; colour, font and photo come from the brand and preset. Examples explain the technique; their words, logos and characters are not copied.

**Common to all references** (we do the same): one technique per video, a palette of 2–3 colours, lots of empty space; the pattern "assemble → long pause on the finished frame → change"; text large and short; the end often loops into the start (seamless loop); videos 4–16 s.

### Text
- **M1. Word by word → paragraph.** Each word first fills the frame in turn, at the end they all settle into a normal headline; small photos may flash around. Hooks and manifestos. Presets: Bold, Editorial, Neutral.
- **M2. Word with pictures inside.** Words move apart and photos or icons slot into the gaps, or pictures fly around a large word. Presets: Editorial, Lifestyle, Street.
- **M3. Word rotator.** A list of words scrolls, one highlighted (brighter or on a plate), next to it a photo changes to match the word. For "who it's for / what we do". Presets: Corporate, Fintech, Editorial.
- **M4. Type beyond the frame.** A word so large it's cropped, slides and reads in full; or a word in perspective. Presets: Bold, Street.
- **M5. Variable font and style changes.** Letters change width, weight or style in place while lines stay readable. Variable font — only from the Design System; decorative style changes — per A11 (one decorative device per video). Presets: Bold, Street.
- **M6. Text on a path.** Text runs along a spiral, loop or circle. Only a short repeated phrase; the main message separately as static text. Presets: Playful, Street, Cultural code (along an ornament line).
- **M7. Interface dialogue.** Text appears as chat bubbles or notification cards. Presets: Fintech, Playful.

### Shapes, line, drawing
- **M8. Shapes reveal the frame.** Circles, blobs, plates grow and merge, revealing text or the next scene; shapes assemble into a logo mark or letter. Presets: any; in Minimal — one soft shape.
- **M9. A line draws in.** Outline shapes fold and turn into a list or diagram; a signature or handwritten mark writes itself. Presets: Health, Minimal, Editorial, Cultural code (ornament).
- **M10. A flat illustration comes alive.** A scene of simple shapes, objects hop in and sway, the colour field changes between scenes. Presets: Playful, Holidays, Food. Illustrations — per the by-presets illustration rule.
- **M11. Frame-by-frame "hand" animation.** A drawing, cards or pixels change frame by frame like a flipbook, a sticker character changes pose. 8–12 fps. Presets: Street, Playful, Food.

### Photo, collage, interface
- **M12. Window and split.** One window frame on a field, the photo inside changes or expands to full frame, the headline stays; or the frame is split by a wipe into two photos. Presets: Editorial, Lifestyle, Real Estate, Food.
- **M13. Collage and stack.** Photos, cut-outs, stickers and newspaper letters layer up, shuffle like cards, torn paper edges. Presets: Street, Editorial, Bold.
- **M14. Interface and numbers.** App cards and phones slide in and scroll; rings and charts draw in, numbers count; portraits in circles orbit. All numbers — per A7. Presets: Fintech, Corporate, Health; product on a field — Lifestyle, Beauty, Food.

### Series and voice
- **M15. Recap series ("the year in numbers").** A Stories series, one fact per Story: a teaser phrase line by line with a stagger ("Your most frequent colour this year…") → the answer large: a counting number or a photo grid. Background shapes (circles, stripes, arcs) flow from Story to Story and carry continuity; the background switches colour from the brand palette by section. Ending — a summary grid of all numbers. For year-end recaps, reports, anniversaries. All numbers — from the client's text (A7). Presets: Fintech, Corporate, Playful, Bold.
- **M16. Karaoke subtitles with a voice wave.** The whole phrase on a flat field, the current word highlighted with a brand-colour plate in time with speech; below — the voice waveform. For quotes from podcasts, interviews, voice testimonials without video. Phrase within subtitle limits, phrase changes on speech pauses. Presets: any.
- **M17. Progress line.** A thin line with a dot travels through the frame or flows from scene to scene; text and circle nodes appear where the dot passes. Links scenes instead of transitions. For steps, timelines, brand presentations. Presets: Minimal, Corporate, Fintech, Editorial.

Additional samples for earlier techniques: M13 — a cut-out person on changing colour fields; food: cut-out dishes and packaging on brand-colour tiles alternating with live cooking shots (Food layer); M14 — a static ad layout breaks into layers and reassembles; onboarding Stories with a question and buttons (where an Instagram sticker replaces the buttons — A10c).

### Product and Beauty
- **M18. The product stays — the world changes.** The product (in hand or on a podium) is still and always in the same place, while backgrounds change around it: views from a plane window, ingredient textures (rice, sand, water, fabric), day and night, flowers opening around a bottle. Hard cuts or on the beat, the headline stays. For "anywhere / for any situation / what it's made of". Presets: Beauty, Lifestyle, Food, Promo.
- **M19. The product assembles.** Ingredients, capsules or fragments fly together into a jar or bottle; the lid comes down from above; ending — the product on a field of the same ingredients. Only from real photos or a 3D model of the product from the client (A9: packaging is not generated). Presets: Beauty, Health, Food.
- **M20. Texture and application.** A macro of skin or texture: a swatch appears and spreads, a drop runs down; then the product name is hand-drawn (M9) or the frame opens like a book cover and shows application on the face. The face may be covered under the A12 exception if the video is about the product. Presets: Beauty, Health.

Also from beauty: words fly in an arc around the product (M6); a route on a map draws in and stops at the products (M9); a set of products on a string or ribbon rides through the frame.

### Cultural code in motion
Status "observation" — refine on the first videos. Calm tempo (like Lifestyle), long pauses. Ornaments and symbols — per the "Cultural code" layer in by-presets (the motifs of the brand's market). Worked example for Central Asian / Kazakh motifs:
- **Living nature:** steppe and grass in the wind, clouds over mountains, water, galloping horses — live video or a slow push-in and layered parallax over a photo (sky, mountains, foreground). The headline sits in a calm zone of sky or steppe.
- **Tea and the table:** steam over a bowl, tea pouring in a stream, hands passing a bowl, a table filling from above (like M18: centre stays, things appear around).
- **Ornament:** draws in as a line from the centre outwards (M9); unfolds symmetrically to 2 or 4 sides; runs as a border ribbon along the frame edge; a rosette rotates slowly (no more than one turn per video); works as a mask — a photo reveals in the shape of the ornament or a traditional architectural symbol. The ornament doesn't deform or "jump".
- Transitions — dissolve, ornament mask or a through-line (M17); no hard cuts or glitch.
For another market, replace the motifs with that culture's own (landscape, ritual, pattern) and keep the same rules.

### Transitions
A transition is part of the technique, not a separate effect: it continues the movement and links the meaning of scenes. One type per video; common ones:
- **Through-element** — the product, window, headline or line stays in place, everything around changes (M12, M17, M18). The cleanest, fits everything.
- **Shape wipe** — a circle, blob or plate grows, covers the frame and opens the next (M8). Playful, Fintech, Health.
- **Colour-field swap** — the background switches on a cut, the hero stays (M13, M15). Bold, Street, Playful.
- **Fly-in** — the camera enters a window, letter, photo or circle and it becomes the next frame. Editorial, Lifestyle, Beauty.
- **Push and page-turn** — cards and screens push each other, the frame opens like a book or a door (M14, M20). Fintech, Corporate, Beauty.
- **Cut on the beat** — an instant change on the strong beat. Bold, Street, Promo.
- **Dissolve** — through opacity, 400–800 ms. Minimal, Lifestyle, Cultural code.

### Choosing
- First the point ("Why motion"), then the technique for it.
- The product and its story → M18–M20.
- Recaps, anniversary, a series of facts in Stories → M15; voice without video → M16; steps and timeline → M17.
- First "Style from practice" (it may hold the brand's technique), then techniques listing the brand's preset, then by content: numbers → M14; a list of audiences or services → M3; manifesto, hook → M1, M4; photo series → M12, M13; holiday, story → M10.
- **Combining techniques.** Up to 7–20 s — no more than two techniques; in videos over 20 s — up to three, one each for hook, middle and ending, connected by a through-element (M17 line, product, headline or background shape). How to combine:
  - **Lead + support:** one technique sets the character, the second helps at points (M18 throughout + M20 at the end).
  - **By parts:** different techniques in different parts (M1 in the hook → M14 with numbers → ending).
  - **Layered in one scene** — only if one works with text and the other with the background (M2 over M13); two techniques on the same element — no.
  - In any combination — one set of curves, one preset tempo, one transition type: the video looks like one system, not a set of effects. The combination is chosen for the point; each technique shows its part of the thought.
  - In a content pillar — the same set of techniques episode after episode. A technique moves at the preset's tempo (table above): M1 in Bold is cut on the beat, in Editorial — slowly from under masks.
- Reading time and zones apply to all techniques: decorative motion stops while text to be read is on screen.
- New motion references the user sends — analyse the same way: video frames at 7 points, technique → an existing M or a new number, with the user's OK.
